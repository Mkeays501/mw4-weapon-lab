INTERACTIVE / RATE MODEL

What if both need the same number of shots?

Explore nominal firing cadence for selected ARs, SMGs and LMGs. This is not measured TTK. Damage, accuracy, burst timing, delays and missed shots are not modeled.

Rate model: (assumed shots − 1) × 60,000 ÷ published RPM. The first shot starts at zero. Only an illustrative constant-cadence model is offered for ARs, SMGs and LMGs; Hyeon Burst and other weapon classes are excluded. Unknown damage prevents a reliable actual TTK calculation.

The source has a few rough edges.

Six unlock ranks disagree between the overview and individual weapon entries. Krait P68’s rate is printed as “260 BPM.” Both are flagged; uncertain numbers stay out of normalized ranking and numeric filters.

The launch guide lists Apex attachments, while the 23 October notes say some beta attachments return later. Names are shown for discovery; their availability and altered combat stats are not assumed.

OFFICIAL SOURCES / VISIBLE GAPS
Definitions, calculations & limitations +

Scope: the 33 base launch weapons listed in the official MW4 guide and confirmed in the launch patch notes. Blueprint variants, unreleased items, Apex conversions and Warzone legacy weapons are not additional base entries.

Specs: fire rate, ammo capacity, reserves and maximum weapon levels are transcribed from the 19 October launch guide. The 24 October patch page was reviewed for context. Neither date means every number was measured in the live game.

Unlock conflicts: both published ranks are retained. A disputed rank sorts last and is excluded by a player-rank limit. “Apex documented” means a name appears in the guide; it does not confirm current availability.

Unknown versus N/A: a dash is a missing reliable measurement. N/A denotes a stat that does not apply, such as a knife’s magazine. Neither is zero. Krait P68’s BPM entry is retained verbatim as a short source label and is not silently converted to RPM.

No accuracy score: in-game stat bars, promotional descriptions and recoil multipliers are not converted into measured hipfire cones or a weapon-control ranking. Exact hipfire, recoil, ADS time, damage and TTK remain unavailable in the reviewed sources.

Rate model: an illustrative constant-rate calculation beginning at the first shot. It excludes damage, ADS, pre-fire and sprint delays, reloads, burst cycles, travel time, misses and network effects. It does not predict which gun kills fastest.

Cross-class highlights: more ammo or a higher RPM can be a useful comparison, but a launcher, sniper, shotgun and rifle fill different roles. Numeric highlights are not a tier list.

Privacy: no accounts or analytics. Filters, selections and model settings are stored only in this browser where storage is available. No third-party fonts or external runtime data calls are added.
